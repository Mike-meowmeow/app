Draft wording for development. The official privacy notice must be supplied and approved by EVSU-Ormoc HRMDO before release.

EVSU-Ormoc HRMDO collects the personal information you provide (such as your name, contact details, education, work experience, eligibility, licenses, and supporting documents) to process your application for employment.

Your information is used for recruitment evaluation, preparation of the official applicant roster, communication about your application, and required records. Access is limited to authorized HR personnel, approvers, and assigned selection committee members.

Retention periods, your rights to access and correct your data, and the contact for privacy concerns will be stated in the approved notice.
